Files analysed: 548   Characters: 3,152,177

Method
------------------------------------------------
Kanripo markup (# comments, <pb:...> page tags, paragraph marks and
indentation) was removed; punctuation was dropped, leaving CJK
characters only. Formal and variant glyphs were merged (e.g. the
Four Treasuries text writes 内 far more often than standard 內, so
both are counted as 內); this map is useful but not exhaustive.
"Bigrams" are pairs of adjacent characters and stand in for words,
since written Chinese has no spaces.
